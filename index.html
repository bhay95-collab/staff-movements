Teams & roles

Build your own service structure: teams, and the roles inside each team.
▾
A team is any grouping you use (a ward, service line, rotation area). A role is a funded position inside a team. Each role has a classification (pay grade) and funded FTE. Funded FTE on roles adds up to the baseline for each classification, so the budget stays in step. Staff lines are then assigned to a team and role in the Staff Register.

Classification budget

Add, rename or remove classifications and set the funded FTE for this service.
▾
Baseline FTE = your funded establishment for that classification. Where roles use a classification, its baseline is added up from those roles automatically; otherwise you can type it here. Ceiling = the most you are permitted to run. Additional approved = extra FTE signed off outside the baseline (e.g. new grant funding) — it is added to the ceiling, not the baseline.
